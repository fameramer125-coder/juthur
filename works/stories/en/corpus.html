The Stories, 1 to 60

Arabic edition: القصص من ١ إلى ٦٠

English edition · version 5 · 5 October 2026

Sixty short stories, each with a small activity and two questions for the guide. They are read in order, one story a day, aloud. This is the English edition of the Arabic corpus, version 5. The Arabic is the reference text, fully vowelled, and every story here links to its Arabic counterpart. A recorded reference reading is added to each story in Listen as the recordings are completed.

al-Mushafaha

Juthur is not a programme against screens. Juthur is a programme to return an absent voice. The screen is not the disease; it is the substitute that arrives when a voice falls silent.

al-Mushafaha is the transmission of language from mouth to ear in the presence of another person: Talqin, then Tardid, then Inshad. Print preserves and voice transmits; the doctrine is not opposed to books, only to a medium that stands in for a person.

This work is delivered by voice, produced and heard, with no moving image in the primary years and below. Assistive technology prescribed for an individual child is exempt without qualification.
