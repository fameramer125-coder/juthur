Closing

خاتمة الكتاب

English edition

The book is closed, and the lamplight stays on the pillow.

On a far shore Sindbad folds his sail, shakes the sand from his shoes, and leaves them by the door. Where the waves end there is a trail of small claw prints heading south, and the cold water covers it and then draws back.

On the wet sand there are many footprints, large and small, some straight and some crooked, some from small claws and some from a donkey’s hoof. A wave comes and wipes out one edge of them, and the other edge goes on shining under the moon. By the sea Talia sits listening; in the desert Ali sets his load on the ground and sits down. Under the lamp lies a closed book, and on its last page there is a patch of white space just right for your drawing. A light breeze passes over the folded pages, and one of them turns by itself and then lies still.

And you who have read, you are accepted as you are, and you are able to grow. One day you will wear shoes bigger than these, and you will walk in them farther than you think. In the morning you will find the door open and the water near.
