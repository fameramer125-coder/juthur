Translator's Threshold

Fascicle II continues the circle-by-circle English rendering of the Four Arcs from the ratified Arabic edition, Tree Stage (12+) of the Juthur project, grounded in Concrete Reading Theory (CRT; Amer, 2024). The conventions of Fascicle I hold unchanged: proprietary terms romanized, never translated; ritual and pull lines bilingual; the author's own English subtitle lines preserved verbatim.

This voyage carries seven Silence-of-the-Question stations, more than the first, as the author's threshold explains, and their body-verbs ascend as a sensory arc of Heat: it flushes, recoils, blazes, glows, warms, softens, widens. Durations and breath counts are preserved exactly.

Draft status: as stated in Fascicle I.

A Threshold · names that burn, names that warm

عَتَبَةٌ

In The Broken Compass , Sindbad learned that names may lie. Now he returns to the same sea, but his compass, pointing inward now, leads him to an archipelago of mirrors: not mirrors that reflect faces, but mirrors that name, fastening to every human being a single word, which becomes his prison.

And the Hungry One has discovered that the fastest way to devour a person is to expose him before the people without mercy, so that he shrinks in shame until he disappears. The Mirror That Names is his instrument. And Marjana, who gave Sindbad a transparent mirror in the first Safar, is its opposite: she reveals a person to himself with gentleness, so that he does not break, but straightens.

The sense of this Safar is Heat , and it rises as an arc: the flush of the cheeks when a secret is exposed; then the heat of anger when truth is spoken without love; then, at last, the warmth of truth when it is spoken with companionship.

The pauses are more frequent here than in the first Safar. They ask where the heat settles in your body: in the cheeks? in the chest? in the throat? They prepare the completion of the method in The Eternal Day.

The Golden Rule of Juthur holds, as in Fascicle I. And Juha's thread remains: "If you find what you are searching for, you will wish you had never searched."

«الْكَشْفُ بِلَا رِفْقَةٍ خِيَانَةٌ.»

Revelation without companionship is betrayal.

خَرِيطَةُ الْقَوْسِ

The Isle of Labels · a mirror that brands a single name

جَزِيرَةُ الْأَلْقَابِ

1 / 1 The Port That Calls Out Names الْمِينَاءُ الَّذِي يُنَادِي بِالْأَسْمَاءِ

Plate · The mirror that names

A mirror on the gate lights a single word on the brow of everyone who enters, and the word follows him like a shadow branded with fire.

مِرْآةٌ عَلَى الْبَوَّابَةِ تُضِيءُ عَلَى جَبِينِ كُلِّ دَاخِلٍ كَلِمَةً وَاحِدَةً تَتْبَعُهُ كَظِلٍّ مَوْسُومٍ بِالنَّارِ

The boat moors at an island veiled in a thin red haze, as though the air itself were fevered. On the gate hangs an enormous mirror, and everyone entering stands before it for a moment; it lights a single word on his brow, "the Coward," "the Failure," "the Traitor", and he walks on with the word following him like a shadow branded with fire.

Sindbad stands before it. He feels a heat climbing from his neck into his cheeks before he has read anything, as though his body knew shame before his mind did. The mirror lights a word… then flickers, and goes out.

Juha: The first time I have ever seen the mirror stammer. Perhaps it cannot find you a single name… you are many.

1 / 2 The Branded الْمَوْسُومُونَ

In the square, people walk with lowered heads, each carrying his word. A woman branded "the Liar" no longer speaks at all, for fear that the mirror might believe itself. A child whose word is "the Stupid One" hides a marvelous drawing behind his back.

Sindbad approaches the child; the child shrinks, his face flushing, on the verge of tears.

The Child: Don't look at me… you will read my name.

Sindbad: I read your drawing first. And whoever draws like this is not stupid.

The word on the child's brow trembles, a slight tremor. It does not vanish, but it fades a little, like an ember passed over by a cool breath. The child lifts his eyes for the first time, not toward the ground, but toward Sindbad's face, as though sensing that he is more than a word.

1 / 3 The Voice of the Hungry One صَوْتُ الْجَائِعِ

At the island's summit stands a building of red glass, and inside it the great Mirror That Names, and behind it, a shadow that breathes. The voice of the Hungry One seeps out, warm as breath on the back of a neck:

Voice of the Hungry One: I no longer need to eat them, Sindbad. It is enough that I name them. A single name is enough for a human being to forget that he is many, so he shrinks, and I devour him while he thanks me for my honesty.

حِينَ تَخَيَّلْتَ كَلِمَةً وَاحِدَةً تُلْصَقُ بِجَبِينِكَ أَمَامَ النَّاسِ، أَيْنَ أَحْسَسْتَ الْحَرَارَةَ أَوَّلًا؟ فِي الْوَجْنَتَيْنِ؟ فِي الْأُذُنَيْنِ؟ فِي أَعْلَى الصَّدْرِ؟ لَاحِظْ مَكَانَهَا، لَا مَعْنَاهَا.

The Isle of Mirrors · revisited · the same truth, two hands

جَزِيرَةُ الْمَرَايَا

2 / 1 The Same Mirror, Another Reading الْمِرْآةُ نَفْسُهَا، قِرَاءَةٌ أُخْرَى

Plate · The searing mirror & the clear one

The same truth: it burns in the hand of the mirror, and it warms in the hand of Marjana.

نَفْسُ الْحَقِيقَةِ: تَحْرِقُ فِي يَدِ الْمِرْآةِ، وَتُدْفِئُ فِي يَدِ مَرْجَانَةَ

They reach the glass island Sindbad knew from before, the Isle of Mirrors. But it is hotter now, and its glass stings the palm. The mirrors here do not show "what is worst," as they did the first time; they show a person a name that others chose for him, and that he then believed.

Marjana: You came back. The first time, I taught you to see your own face. Now I will teach you what is harder: to see another's face without burning it.

2 / 2 The Difference Between Two Revealers الْفَرْقُ بَيْنَ كَاشِفَيْنِ

A woman stands before the Mirror That Names; it lights the word "the Selfish One." The woman collapses, flushing to her ears, and flees. Marjana follows her, sits beside her, and quietly raises the transparent mirror before her.

Marjana: The mirror told half the truth. You are afraid to give, lest what remains be lost. That is not selfishness, that is an old hunger that was never fed. Come, let us look at it together.

The woman grows calm. The word on the mirror fades, not because it was denied, but because it was spoken with companionship.

Sindbad (to himself) : The same truth… it burned in the hand of the mirror, and it warmed in the hand of Marjana. The difference is not in the truth, but in the hand that carries it.

2 / 3 Sindbad's Trial تَجْرِبَةُ سِنْدِبَادَ

Marjana tells him to take the transparent mirror from his pocket. He turns toward Juha, and is tempted to expose a secret he knows about him. A heat rises in his chest, almost delicious: the heat of one who holds the power to wound.

Juha: I see the heat in your eyes. The truth in your hand right now is a sword. What will you do with it?

Sindbad: I will return it to its sheath. I am not a judge.

The heat in his chest goes out, and a gentle coolness follows it, as though something had been fevered, and then grew still.

The Isle of Cruel Counsel · truth held by the blade

جَزِيرَةُ النَّصِيحَةِ الْجَارِحَةِ

3 / 1 The Market of Counselors سُوقُ النَّاصِحِينَ

Plate · Truth gripped by the blade

They grip the truth by its blade and stab others with it, then they call the blood honesty.

يُمْسِكُونَ الْحَقِيقَةَ مِنَ النَّصْلِ وَيَطْعَنُونَ بِهَا غَيْرَهُمْ، ثُمَّ يُسَمُّونَ الدَّمَ صِدْقًا

A clamorous island, hot as an open oven, where men stand on platforms shouting: "I tell the truth to your face and I flatter no one!" Around every counselor a crowd applauds, and on the ground beneath the platforms, people bleed from honest words spoken without mercy.

A red-faced counselor screams at a girl: "You are a failure, and I say it out of love!" The girl collapses. The crowd applauds: "How brave he is! How honest!"

Sindbad: They hold the sword by its blade and swing it anyway, so it is the listeners who bleed.

Juha: Honesty without mercy is not courage; it is cruelty wearing the robe of virtue. The easiest thing in the world is to wound, and call your wound a truth.

3 / 2 Sindbad's Own Heat حَرَارَةُ سِنْدِبَادَ نَفْسِهِ

Sindbad meets a counselor about whom he knows a real fault. A heat rises in his chest, the heat of honest anger, the desire to throw the man's own face at him before everyone. He opens his mouth…

Then he sees the counselor's eyes trembling behind his shouting. He closes his mouth.

Sindbad: I was going to tell you your truth in front of the people, and put out your face with it. But I saw that you shout because you yourself were branded, long ago. The wounded wound.

The counselor's face flushes, not with anger this time, but with something resembling the first shame in years.

The Silence of the Question

ثَمَانِ ثَوَانٍ · Eight seconds

نَفَسَانِ… · Two breaths… then notice

When you held a truth you could wound with, against someone who had wronged you, where did the heat gather? In the fist? In the jaw? In the throat? Stay with it a moment, and then, as Sindbad did, let it go.

حِينَ مَلَكْتَ حَقِيقَةً تَجْرَحُ بِهَا مَنْ أَسَاءَ إِلَيْكَ، أَيْنَ تَجَمَّعَتِ الْحَرَارَةُ؟ فِي قَبْضَةِ الْيَدِ؟ فِي الْفَكِّ؟ فِي الْحَلْقِ؟ ابْقَ مَعَهَا لَحْظَةً دُونَ أَنْ تُطْلِقَهَا.

It blazes يَشْتَعِلُ

The Isle of Public Confession · the heat at its peak

جَزِيرَةُ الِاعْتِرَافِ الْعَلَنِيِّ

4 / 1 The Red Arena السَّاحَةُ الْحَمْرَاءُ

Plate · The arena of embers

People expose themselves willingly on embers they call purification, and Sindbad stands between them and the mirror.

يَفْضَحُ النَّاسُ أَنْفُسَهُمْ طَوْعًا عَلَى جَمْرٍ يُسَمُّونَهُ تَطَهُّرًا، فَيَقِفُ سِنْدِبَادُ بَيْنَهُمْ وَالْمِرْآةِ

A circular arena, its floor of dim, glowing embers, a pulpit at its center. The law here is one: every person must climb up and confess the worst that is in him before everyone, "to be purified." Whoever confesses is applauded, then he steps down having lost something of himself that does not return.

Juha: Look, they call it purification. But embers do not purify; embers eat. Here the people expose themselves willingly, so the Hungry One does not need to unmask them, they unmask themselves for him.

4 / 2 The Mirror Names · In Public الْمِرْآةُ تُسَمِّي، عَلَنًا

At the top of the arena hangs the great Mirror That Names, and behind it the Hungry One. Every time someone confesses, the mirror lights his name above his head for all to see, and the confessor shrinks, and grows smaller.

A woman climbs up and begins to confess, weeping. Sindbad climbs up behind her, not to confess, but to stand between her and the mirror.

Sindbad: I will not stop you from speaking. But not for them, for me alone, or for someone who loves you. Confession is not a spectacle. Whoever loves you hears your secret to carry it with you, not to watch you break.

The mirror trembles, and its glow dims, as though something inside it had hesitated. For the first time, a woman climbs up and comes down without a name lighting above her head. She passes Sindbad, and in her eyes is something that was not there before: not joy, but the relief of one who carried her own secret herself and did not hand it over to the crowd.

4 / 3 Confronting the Hungry One مُوَاجَهَةُ الْجَائِعِ

Voice of the Hungry One: You have ruined my feast. They were serving themselves up to me on a plate of embers.

Sindbad: Because you convinced them that exposing oneself is courage. But courage is to know your secret, not to hang it above your head.

Sindbad touches the great mirror, and his own image is reflected on it, not a single name, but a whole face, changing. The mirror cracks: one thin fissure.

The Market of Secrets · the heat cools toward truth

سُوقُ الْأَسْرَارِ

5 / 1 The Merchandise Is People's Secrets الْبِضَاعَةُ أَسْرَارُ النَّاسِ

A covered market, cooler than the previous islands, where secrets are sold in small glass vials, each glowing with its owner's color. A skillful merchant buys secrets cheaply from the desperate, and sells them to anyone who wants to wound another with them.

The Merchant: One secret is enough to break your enemy. Buy, for truth is a weapon.

Sindbad: Truth is not a weapon for sale. Truth is a trust, and whoever sells another's trust has stolen twice.

5 / 2 Sindbad's Vial قَارُورَةُ سِنْدِبَادَ

Sindbad finds a vial that carries his own secret, the secret of his mother who left, and her question he never understood. He trembles. He could buy it, and hide it forever.

Marjana: You can bury it. Or you can open it, and look at it with me.

Sindbad opens the vial. No fire comes out of it, only a quiet warmth, like the heat of a hand on a shoulder. A secret revealed with companionship does not burn.

Sindbad: I feared my secret because I carried it alone. Revelation without companionship is betrayal, even if you are the one revealing yourself to yourself, alone.

Marjana and the Two Faces · the revealer revealed

مَرْجَانَةُ وَالْوَجْهَانِ

6 / 1 Who Is Marjana? مَنْ تَكُونُ مَرْجَانَةُ؟

Plate · Two faces, one woman

In a single mirror, two faces: the harsh Marjana who was, and the gentle Marjana who became, and both are her.

فِي مِرْآةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ وَجْهَانِ: مَرْجَانَةُ الْقَاسِيَةُ الَّتِي كَانَتْ، وَمَرْجَانَةُ الرَّفِيقَةُ الَّتِي صَارَتْ، وَالِاثْنَانِ هِيَ

Sindbad follows Marjana to a cave, and discovers her secret: Marjana was once herself the Mirror That Names. She used to expose people without mercy, wrecking those she loved in the name of honesty, until she lost the dearest person she had, by a true word spoken at the wrong time.

Marjana: I made the mirrors that name. Then I saw what they did. So I broke my mirror, and from its shards I made a transparent mirror that reflects nothing, to teach myself to listen before I judge. The Hungry One uses my old craft. And I atone for it.

6 / 2 The Two Faces in One Mirror الْوَجْهَانِ فِي مِرْآةٍ وَاحِدَةٍ

Marjana raises her transparent mirror between herself and Sindbad, and in it he sees two faces: the face of the old, harsh Marjana, and her face now. And both are her.

Sindbad: You were not wicked and then good. You were hungry for truth, and then you learned to feed it gently.

Marjana: As you are, Sindbad. The Hungry One inside you is not your enemy. He is you, when you forget companionship.

The Exit · the name you choose for yourself

الْخُرُوجُ: الِاسْمُ الَّذِي تَخْتَارُهُ

7 / 1 The Last Mirror الْمِرْآةُ الْأَخِيرَةُ

Plate · The mirror finds no single name

The mirror fails to name him, and on the horizon looms a heavy land where time moves slowly.

تَعْجِزُ الْمِرْآةُ عَنْ تَسْمِيَتِهِ، وَفِي الْأُفُقِ تَلُوحُ أَرْضٌ ثَقِيلَةٌ بَطِيئَةُ الزَّمَانِ

Sindbad returns to the gate where the Mirror That Names hangs. He stands before it. It tries to light a single name above his head, flickers, then goes out entirely. For whoever has learned that he is "many" cannot be held by a single word.

Voice of the Hungry One: I will find no name for you.

Sindbad: Because I will not give you one. I will carry all my names: the frightened and the brave, the wounding and the gentle, the hungry and the filled. I am all of this, and none of it alone.

7 / 2 Marjana's Gift هَدِيَّةُ مَرْجَانَةَ

Marjana closes his fingers around the transparent mirror he has carried since the first voyage.

Marjana: Keep it. Not to see your face, you learned that long ago. But to carry it when you meet someone branded, and show him that he is more than his word. You are now able to reveal with companionship.

On the horizon glimmers a strange land, heavy, as though time there walks barefoot on sinking sand. No heat there, and no names that burn; something else waits, slower, deeper, as though the sea itself sighs before it.

Juha: The Land Beyond Time. There the names do not lie and the mirrors do not burn… there, loss itself grows heavy.

صَوْتُ الْجَائِعِ: «الْكَلِمَاتُ لَمْ تَعُدْ تَنْفَعُ مَعَكَ. سَأَنْتَظِرُكَ حَيْثُ يَثْقُلُ الْقَلْبُ فَلَا يَقْدِرُ عَلَى الْكَلَامِ.»

Voice of the Hungry One: "Words no longer work on you. I will wait for you where the heart grows so heavy it cannot speak."

A Whisper to the Companion

هَمْسٌ لِلْمُرَافِقِ

Read after, never during.

For the teacher, parent or companion, as in Fascicle I: read after the journey, never in its midst.

A note on secrets. These pauses can bring a real secret to the surface. Listen quietly, do not press for detail, and thank the child for trusting you. Never promise to keep a secret that touches the child's safety; follow your school's safeguarding procedure.

The Key of Heat

The whole arc is about Heat as a moral compass: the same truth burns or warms according to the hand that carries it. The gathering lesson: "Revelation without companionship is betrayal", do not conceal the truth, but do not hurl it like an ember. And a name is never the person: whoever is fastened to one word has been made smaller than he is.

The Gradation of the Seals

Seven pauses ascend sensorially: it flushes (the shame of exposure) ← it recoils ← it blazes (the peak of anger) ← it glows ← it warms ← it softens ← it widens (one who has accepted that he is many). Let the reader trace where the heat travels in his body from one seal to the next.

Marjana and the Hungry One: Two Faces of One Truth

The key of the epic is that Marjana was once herself "the Mirror That Names"; the cruel revealer and the gentle revealer are not two persons, but two phases of one human being. And so with the Hungry One: "he is you, when you forget companionship." Do not present this as a direct lesson; let the reader discover that cruelty and mercy dwell in the same hand.

«نَفْسُ الْحَقِيقَةِ تَحْرِقُ فِي يَدٍ، وَتُدْفِئُ فِي أُخْرَى.»

The same truth burns in one hand and warms in another.
