العدّاد الحيّ لورشة المعايرة: أدخل لكل عيّنة ولكل جولة عدد المقيِّمين الذين اختاروا كل مستوى رئيسي، وعدد من كتب U (غير قابلة للتقييم). الـ U بيانات ناقصة لا رتبة منخفضة، ولا تدخل في أي نسبة اتفاق. لكل لغة شبكتها، وما تكتبه يبقى في هذا المتصفح حتى تمسحه.

Workshop 1 rates major levels only. For each sample and each round, enter how many raters chose each major level, and in the last column how many circled U (unratable). U is missing data, never a low rating: it is counted and shown, and left out of every agreement figure. Each language has its own grid; what you type is kept in this browser until you clear it.

Each row must add up to this number. A row that does not is flagged.

Results ·

Read these figures for what they are. They describe one small room on six constructed samples. With two to four raters a single changed rating moves every percentage a long way. Krippendorff’s α on six samples reflects how far apart the samples’ levels were built to be as much as it reflects the raters: samples spread from Novice to Superior give a high α even when raters disagree on each one. It is shown as a secondary index and is left blank, never shown as 1, when every rating is identical. The per-sample rows above are the primary display.

Exact agreement = share of rater pairs choosing the same major level. Adjacent = within one major level. Distance = average gap, in major levels, between all rater pairs on a sample. The Round 1 / Round 2 comparison uses only the samples that at least two raters rated in both rounds, and says how many those were. Descriptive statistics for this room only; not a benchmark.
