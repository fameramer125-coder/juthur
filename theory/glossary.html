1 · The naming rule

Amended by the author, 27 September 2026. This amendment replaces the two-name rule of 18 August 2026.

One name everywhere: CRT · Concrete Reading Theory (نَظَرِيَّةُ القِرَاءَةِ المَلْمُوسَة). It is the name under which the project’s first peer-reviewed paper was published in Frontiers in Education (28 September 2026, doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1880655), and it is now used on this site and in books, instruments, charters, classroom material and new manuscripts.

Earlier names. TRT (Tactile Reading Theory) and TRF (Tactile Reading Framework) name the same theory. They stay as written in documents already issued and in manuscripts already submitted under them; nothing new uses them.

Retired: Tangible Reading Framework · Tangible Reading Theory. These are corrected silently wherever they appear.

2 · Standing rule: read before using any term below

None of the terms in this addendum is part of Amer (2024). The 2024 glossary is closed and published. Every term here entered the programme in 2026 and is cited Amer (2026), or described as an unpublished CRT extension.

Citing any of these as Amer (2024) is a false citation in a body of work that is under peer review. It will be checked, and it will cost more than the convenience it buys. The 2024 terms and the 2026 terms are listed separately for exactly this reason and must never be merged into one table.

The formatting rules of the master glossary carry over without change: full tashkeel on every Arabic form · simplified international romanisation · proprietary terms are never translated in running text, only glossed on first use · first use gives Arabic + romanized + definition + citation, and later uses give the romanized form alone.

3 · The delivery doctrine

Arabic (fully vowelled) Romanised English gloss Citation
الْمُشَافَهَةُ al-Mushafaha Oral transmission, the governing delivery doctrine of the Juthur programme Amer (2026)

First-use template

al-Mushafaha (الْمُشَافَهَةُ; Amer, 2026), the transmission of language mouth to ear in the presence of another person, and the delivery doctrine governing the Juthur programme,

A required guard. al-Mushafaha is not a seventh principle of CRT. The six principles, Tazamun · Tadarruj · Takhsis · Nafidha · Shumul · Takamul, remain six and are closed. The doctrine sits above the theory as its condition of delivery: CRT accounts for how reading is acquired, al-Mushafaha governs how it is transmitted. Any document that presents it as a principle of the theory must be corrected before it is issued.

4 · The three mechanisms

Arabic (fully vowelled) Romanised English gloss Citation
التَّلْقِين Talqin Transmission · the adult says it first Amer (2026)
التَّرْدِيد Tardid Fixing · the child returns it across days until it is held Amer (2026)
الإِنْشَاد Inshad Intensification · melody and rhythm carry what speech cannot hold Amer (2026)

Always in this order: Talqin · Tardid · Inshad. Receive, return, sing. The sequence is part of the definition and is not reordered for style.

7 · Superseded names

Retired Replaced by Date
صُنْدُوقُ الذِّكْرَيَاتِ · Sunduq al-Dhikrayat · Memory Box الدُّرْج · ad-Durj 18 August 2026
الصِّوَان · aṣ-Ṣiwan (proposed, never adopted) الدُّرْج · ad-Durj 18 August 2026
الْكُرَةُ الْمَسْحُورَة · The Enchanted Ball الْكُرَةُ الْعَجِيبَة · The Wondrous Ball 18 August 2026

aṣ-Ṣiwan was rejected before adoption: in Gulf and Levantine usage the word is bound to the funeral tent (صِوَان الْعَزَاء) ahead of its root ص-و-ن. It appears here only so it is not proposed again.

الْمَسْحُورَة was retired on objection. مَسْحُورَة is a passive participle meaning a thing upon which sihr has been worked; sihr is forbidden in Islam, so the name sits wrongly against a Qur’anic-content edition, puts the ball in the passive voice when the whole mechanism depends on the child acting, and reads against al-Mushafaha, which exists to remove the dazzling intermediary. الْعَجِيبَة keeps the child’s wonder: عَجَب is a commended register in the tradition (عجائب المخلوقات). See the decision note for the full reasoning.

الْمُشَافَهَةُ

جُذُور لَيْسَتْ مَشْرُوعًا ضِدَّ الشَّاشَةِ؛ جُذُور مَشْرُوعٌ لِإِعَادَةِ الصَّوْتِ الْغَائِبِ. النَّصُّ الْكَامِلُ فِي الْمِيثَاقِ.

Juthur is not a programme against screens; it is a programme to return an absent voice. The full statement is in the Charter.
