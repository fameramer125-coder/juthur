0 · The naming rule — standing and final

Decided by the author, 18 August 2026. This rule does not get re-opened.

Where Name Arabic
Research papers and manuscripts submitted for publication TRF — Tactile Reading Framework إِطَار
Everywhere else — books, instruments, charters, classroom material, the website, family and teacher material, decision records TRT — Tactile Reading Theory نَظَرِيَّة

Dead, in every context: CRT · Concrete Reading Theory · Tangible Reading Framework · Tangible Reading Theory. These are corrected silently wherever they appear.

Why the split, recorded so it is not undone by someone who does not know the reason. A manuscript that calls the work a theory invites the reviewer's next question — where is the falsifiable prediction, where is the discriminating evidence — and invites it in the first line of the abstract. Framework claims exactly what the work does claim: an organising structure with instruments and a method. It is the smaller claim, and the smaller claim survives review. Outside the journals there is no such adversary, and theory is the name the work was published and built under; changing it there would fracture a decade of material for nothing.

So: the register changes, the content does not. TRF and TRT name the same thing, and no document should ever suggest otherwise or attempt to distinguish them substantively.

Every document issued on 18 August 2026 uses TRT and is correct — none of them is a research paper.

Standing rule — read before using any term below

None of the terms in this addendum is part of Amer (2024). The 2024 glossary is closed and published. Every term here entered the programme in 2026 and is cited Amer (2026), or described as an unpublished TRT extension.

Citing any of these as Amer (2024) is a false citation in a body of work that is under peer review. It will be checked, and it will cost more than the convenience it buys. The 2024 terms and the 2026 terms are listed separately for exactly this reason and must never be merged into one table.

The formatting rules of the master glossary carry over without change: full tashkeel on every Arabic form · simplified international romanization · proprietary terms are never translated in running text, only glossed on first use · first use gives Arabic + romanized + definition + citation, and later uses give the romanized form alone.

A required guard. al-Mushafaha is not a seventh principle of TRT. The six principles — Tazamun · Tadarruj · Takhsis · Nafidha · Shumul · Takamul — remain six and are closed. aṣ-Ṣiwan was rejected before adoption: in Gulf and Levantine usage the word is bound to the funeral tent (صِوَان الْعَزَاء) ahead of its root ص-و-ن. It appears here only so it is not proposed again.

الْمَسْحُورَة was retired on objection. مَسْحُورَة is a passive participle — a thing upon which sihr has been worked — which is a prohibition in Islam, sits wrongly against a Qur'anic-content edition, puts the ball in the passive voice when the whole mechanism depends on the child acting, and reads against al-Mushafaha, which exists to remove the dazzling intermediary. الْعَجِيبَة keeps the child's wonder: عَجَب is a commended register in the tradition (عجائب المخلوقات). See the decision note for the full reasoning.

الْمُشَافَهَةُ

جُذُور لَيْسَ مَشْرُوعًا ضِدَّ الشَّاشَةِ؛ جُذُور مَشْرُوعٌ لِإِعَادَةِ الصَّوْتِ الْغَائِبِ. فَالشَّاشَةُ لَيْسَتِ الدَّاءَ، بَلْ هِيَ الْبَدِيلُ الَّذِي يَحْضُرُ حِينَ يَسْكُتُ صَوْتٌ.

وَالْمُشَافَهَةُ نَقْلُ اللُّغَةِ مِنْ فَمٍ إِلَى أُذُنٍ بِحُضُورِ إِنْسَان: تَلْقِينٌ، ثُمَّ تَرْدِيدٌ، ثُمَّ إِنْشَادٌ. الْكِتَابُ يَحْفَظُ، وَالشَّيْخُ يُقْرِئُ؛ فَلَيْسَتِ الْمُشَافَهَةُ ضِدَّ الْوَرَقِ، وَإِنَّمَا ضِدَّ أَنْ يَحُلَّ الْوَسِيطُ مَحَلَّ الْإِنْسَان.

يُقَدَّمُ هَذَا الْعَمَلُ صَوْتًا — إِنْتَاجًا وَسَمَاعًا — بِلَا صُورَةٍ مُتَحَرِّكَةٍ فِي الْمَرْحَلَةِ الِابْتِدَائِيَّةِ وَمَا قَبْلَهَا. وَتُسْتَثْنَى التِّقْنِيَةُ الْمُسَاعِدَةُ الْمَوْصُوفَةُ لِطِفْلٍ بِعَيْنِهِ، بِلَا قَيْدٍ.

al-Mushafaha

Juthur is not a programme against screens. Juthur is a programme to return an absent voice. The screen is not the disease; it is the substitute that arrives when a voice stops.

al-Mushafaha is the transmission of language mouth to ear, in the presence of another person: Talqin, then Tardid, then Inshad. Print carries and voice transmits — the doctrine is not opposed to books, only to a medium standing in for a person.

This work is delivered by voice, produced and heard, with no moving image in the primary years and below. Assistive technology prescribed for an individual child is exempt without qualification.
